# The ceiling survives exhaustion

Search for exceptional contractions that would break the bound. Eighteen Higgs contents enumerated
across SU(5), SO(10), E6 and Pati-Salam. No exception. Two things came out of the search that are
worth more than the confirmation.

## Result

Eighteen combinations; six spectrum-splitting; twelve excluded; no splitting model above its
baseline except one, which carries exactly one extra relation.

## An error in my own code

The operator 10_i 5b_j 45b_H had been recorded as forbidden, on the reasoning that a 45 acting on a
5b yields no singlet. That is wrong: 10 x 5b = 5 + 45, and 45 x 45 contains a singlet. The operator
exists with coefficients +1 and -3 — the Georgi-Jarlskog factor, whose whole purpose is to split M_d
from M_e. The operator count in an early table happened to be right; the stated reason was not.

## The trap I nearly fell into

SO(10) with 120 + 126 has a coefficient matrix of rank two and satisfies M_u + 2 M_d + M_e = 0
identically. For a while that looked like a counterexample. It is not:

> Only a *scalar* proportionality between two sectors equates eigenvalues. A general linear relation
> with all coefficients non-zero is absorbed by the freedom of the other matrices and produces no
> relation among observables.

Anyone auditing predictivity by counting linear relations among mass matrices will overcount by one
here. That is a trap worth stating, because a referee will otherwise fall into it on the way to
rejecting the paper.

## What the enumeration does not cover

Non-renormalisable operators (suppressed by the cutoff, hence a prediction rather than a gap);
relations among vacuum expectation values from soft terms; Higgs irreps outside the enumerated list.
