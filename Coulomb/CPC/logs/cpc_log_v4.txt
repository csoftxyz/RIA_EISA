E. Stability of the radius formula near q -> 1 (reference: 30 digits)
==============================================================================
difference form: rho^(d-1) = (d-2)(r1-r0)/(r0^(2-d)-r1^(2-d))
factored form  : rho = r0 [ (d-2) q^(d-2) (q-1) / (q^(d-2)-1) ]^(1/(d-1))
     q-1     difference       factored
   1e-02      7.238e-16      6.100e-17
   1e-03      3.837e-15      6.429e-17
   1e-04      6.448e-14      8.862e-17
   1e-06      8.110e-12      2.211e-17
   1e-08      5.511e-10      1.667e-17
the difference form loses roughly one digit per decade of q-1;
the factored form stays at round-off. In d = 3 both reduce to rho^2 = r_n r_{n+1}.
